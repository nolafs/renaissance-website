4Divinity approached Renaissance to lead the PR & organic influencer global campaign for a brand-new unannounced IP, Guns of Eschaton.

{{< imageSize image="/images/case-studies/goe1.png" align="left"  alt="goe1" width="578" height="446" processing="resize">}}

We are focused on delivering a clear campaign from announcement through until it’s launch on PC & console in 2026 & for the first major beat of announcing the game we achieved the following results.

{{< imageSize image="/images/case-studies/goe2.png" align="left"  alt="goe2" width="578" height="312" processing="resize">}}

Post 7-days of the announcement & trailer we had secured **238 articles with** a **UVPM of 545.6 million**. Of those **50** are from Tier 1 media with highlights including **Terra Game On (32.3m)**, **GameStar (11.1m)**, **PC Gamer (10.8m), GamesRadar+ (10.5m)** & **3D Juegos (7.3m)**.

{{< imageSize image="/images/case-studies/goe3.png" align="left"  alt="goe3" width="424" height="374" processing="resize">}}

We also reached out to a very small group of targeted, trusted creators ahead of the announcement, allowing us to control any potential leaks. At the time of writing, we have seen over **3.3 million** views from a subscriber base of **67.7 million** across YouTube, Twitch & TikTok. (All organic)

{{< imageSize image="/images/case-studies/goe4.png" align="left"  alt="goe4" width="423" height="387" processing="resize">}}

Ahead of Gamescom 2026, Renaissance PR developed & managed an **exclusive London preview event** for
4Divinity’s Guns of Eschaton. The objective was to introduce the game to a carefully selected group of influential games media & content creators through meaningful **hands-on access, developer interviews** & a memorable experience aligned with the title’s distinctive world.

{{< imageSize image="/images/case-studies/goe5.jpg" align="left"  alt="goe5" width="516" height="774" processing="resize">}}

The event was delivered through **three intimate two-hour sessions**, giving attendees substantial gameplay time alongside a structured introduction to the game’s vision & lore. Renaissance coordinated the guest program, interview schedule & on-site media management, creating **dedicated opportunities** for press conversations while
allowing creators the **flexibility to explore the game & capture** informed impressions.

{{< imageSize image="/images/case-studies/goe6.jpg" align="left"  alt="goe6" width="773" height="516" processing="resize">}}

The event secured 21 confirmed attendees across high-profile international media & creators, including **IGN,
Eurogamer, Metro, Radio Times, GamesIndustry.biz, EDGE, PC Gamer, Outside Xtra, Jackfrags** & **MrRoflWaffles**. 

{{< imageSize image="/images/case-studies/goe7.png" align="left"  alt="goe7" width="586" height="396" processing="resize">}}

This concentrated format brought influential voices into direct contact with the game & its team establishing a strong foundation for embargoed previews, creator content & continued advocacy around Gamescom.
