Renaissance PR worked with Curve Games on Wax Heads covering the games release date announcement through until the launch on PC & consoles, covering both media & content creators.

{{< imageSize image="/images/case-studies/wh1.png" align="left"  alt="wh1" width="602" height="487" processing="resize">}}

Campaign coverage has seen a total of **428 articles** with a combined reach of **1.1 billion UVPM.** At the time of writing, we had tracked 88 reviews.

{{< imageSize image="/images/case-studies/wh2.png" align="left"  alt="wh2" width="378" height="493" processing="resize">}}

**Highlights included IGN (80m),GameSpot (33m), Polygon (11.8m), Pocket Tactics (7.5m), The Gamer (10.8m) & Metro (12.7m) to name just a few.**

{{< imageSize image="/images/case-studies/wh3.png" align="left"  alt="wh3" width="327" height="462" processing="resize">}}

With regards to the organic content creators, we generated **over 422 pieces of content across YouTube, Twitch & TikTok** from a subscriber base of **over 9.8 million**, resulting in over 1**.3 million views** at the time of writing for the 7 days post launch.

{{< imageSize image="/images/case-studies/wh4.png" align="left"  alt="wh4" width="780" height="538" processing="resize">}}

All of this was achieved organically via clear curation targeting the right media & influencers who would be best suited to cover the game.
